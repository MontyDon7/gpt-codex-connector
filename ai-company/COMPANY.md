## 2. Business model

Lumenhall is an **AI management consultancy for mid-size firms (200 to 2,000 employees)**. Work is delivered by **human senior consultants plus AI agents** ("digital colleagues") working under named human owners. Revenue comes from three service lines (§3): project-based advisory, AI implementation, and a recurring **managed-ops retainer** that keeps delivered agents and processes running.

- **Land and expand:** first engagement is usually a Compass Spark or Forge Pilot; target is a Keep retainer attached to every Forge Build/Scale and ≥40% of Compass Programmes.
- **Leverage model:** each human delivery consultant is supported by roughly 0.5 AI agents in FY2026 rising to 0.5 to 0.65 by FY2028 (research, analysis, drafting, QA, monitoring). Agents never replace human accountability (§9.4).
- **Ideal client:** UK or US mid-market firms in financial services, healthcare services, logistics, manufacturing, retail and professional services, with a board-level sponsor and a defined operational pain point.

## 4. Delivery model

- Target billable utilisation (delivery humans): 70% blended (Analyst/Consultant 80%, Manager 65%, Principal 50%, Partner 35%).
- Staffing pyramid (delivery): Partner 5%, Principal 10%, Manager 20%, Senior Consultant 30%, Consultant 25%, Analyst 10%.

- Typical engagement team (Forge Build): 1 Principal (part-time), 1 Manager, 2 Senior Consultants, 1 Consultant, 3 to 5 AI agents.

## 7. Billing and commercial terms

- **Compass and Forge (fixed fee):** milestone billing, 30% on signature, 40% at mid-point milestone, 30% on acceptance. Expenses billed at cost, capped at 5% of fee.
- **Keep:** invoiced monthly in advance, 12-month minimum term, annual uplift CPI + 2% (cap 7%).
- **Change orders:** at rate card (§3.5), signed before work starts.
- **Payment terms:** net 30 days. Late payment interest at 8% over base rate (UK Late Payment Act). VAT 20% added for UK clients. US sales tax handled per state.
- **Collections ladder:** reminder day 31, call day 38, finance-head escalation day 45, legal letter day 60.
- **Targets:** DSO ≤ 45 days; bad debt < 1% of revenue; invoices issued within 2 business days of milestone acceptance.
- **Revenue recognition:** fixed fee on percentage-of-completion; Keep straight-line monthly.

## 8. Key assumptions

1. Demand: UK mid-market AI adoption continues to grow; budget owners buy outcomes, not hours.
2. Win rate 30%, lead-to-qualified 25%, sales cycle ≈75 days (§5.3).
3. Utilisation 70% blended; attrition 14% (humans) in Y1 to 11% in Y3; time-to-hire 45 days (human), 5 days (agent provisioning).
4. AI model and compute unit costs fall about 15% a year; agent leverage rises accordingly.
5. Employer on-costs 28% of base salary. London salaries per §6.2. Department budgets per §5.4.
6. US launch gated on ≥£10m ARR run-rate and ISO 27001 certification (achieved by end FY2026).
7. Compliance baseline: UK GDPR and DPA 2018, ISO 27001 and Cyber Essentials Plus (FY2026), SOC 2 Type II (FY2027, before US launch), Companies House and HMRC obligations, alignment with the EU AI Act and UK AI regulatory principles for client AI systems.
8. No M&A. No client concentration above 15% of revenue (policy).

### 9.4 AI agent governance

- Every AI agent has a named human accountable owner and is registered in the agent register (Data and IT Security).
- Agents may draft, analyse, monitor and propose. Agents may **not** sign contracts, release client deliverables without human QA, approve payments above £10,000, make final hiring or termination decisions, or notify regulators.
- Agent actions on client data follow the data-processing agreement and least-privilege access; agent outputs used in client deliverables are logged for audit.
- Material changes to client-facing agents require an AI risk review (`product.ai_risk_review_requested` then `legal.ai_risk_review_completed`).
