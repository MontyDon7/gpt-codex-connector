## 3. Pricing and unit economics

The People department is a cost centre with no external price list. Its economics are internal unit costs and the leverage the department provides to a company growing from 38 to 230 humans (COMPANY.md §6).

### 3.1 Hiring volume

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Net additions | 38 | 72 | 120 |
| Average humans | 29 | 74 | 170 |
| Attrition assumption (COMPANY.md §8.3) | 14% | 12.5% | 11% |
| Leavers to backfill (attrition x average) | 4 | 9 | 19 |
| **Gross hires (net additions + backfills)** | **42** | **81** | **139** |
| Agency-placed hires | 3 | 8 | 20 |
| Referral hires | 3 | 20 | 45 |

Y1 assumes the company starts FY2026 with no People infrastructure and hires its 38 year-end staff over the year, mostly through founder and network sourcing, which is why agency use is low. The 12 department heads (COMPANY.md §9.2) account for the agency-placed Y1 hires.

### 3.2 Cost per hire

Cost per hire = (agency + referral + ATS and job boards + screening + Talent Acquisition staff cost + allocated recruiting time) / gross hires. Allocated recruiting time is 30% of CPO time in FY2026 and FY2028, and 30% of CPO and Talent Acquisition Partner time in FY2027 (the Talent Acquisition Partner owns requisition-to-offer from 2027-04-01).

| Direct recruitment spend | 59 | 198 | 556 |
| Talent Acquisition staff cost | 0.0 | 59.5 | 160.9 |
| Allocated leadership and partner time | 55.7 | 87.2 | 55.7 |
| **Total hiring cost** | 114.7 | 344.7 | 772.6 |
| Gross hires | 42 | 81 | 139 |
| **Fully loaded cost per hire (£)** | 2,730 | 4,255 | 5,558 |
| Direct-spend-only cost per hire (£) | 1,405 | 2,444 | 4,000 |
| Average fee per agency hire (£) | 13,333 | 16,000 | 19,000 |

Benchmark context: UK professional-services cost per hire typically £3k to £6k with a blended agency share; the modeled range sits at the low end because most hires are direct or referred and the target time-to-hire is 45 days (COMPANY.md §8.3). Agency fee is 15% / 18% / 20% of average first-year base of about £89k / £89k / £95k for agency-placed roles.

### 3.3 Cost of the vacancy versus cost of the fix

A vacant billable seat costs revenue. Revenue per average human FTE is £207k / £264k / £272k (COMPANY.md §5.2). Utilisation is 70% blended (§8.3), so the revenue at risk per delivery-consultant vacancy day is lower than the average, but the average is a useful upper bound.

| | FY2026 | FY2027 | FY2028 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue per average human FTE (£k) | 207 | 264 | 272 |
| Revenue at risk per day of vacancy (£) = revenue / 365 | 567 | 723 | 745 |
| Cost of a 45-day vacancy (£k) | 25.5 | 32.5 | 33.5 |
| Cost of a 30-day slip beyond target (£k) | 17.0 | 21.7 | 22.4 |
| Typical agency fee at the model's average (£k) | 13.3 | 16.0 | 19.0 |

Reading: where an agency saves 30 or more days on a billable delivery role, the fee is repaid by recovered revenue. For non-billable roles the case rests on capability, not revenue, and agencies are used only for senior or scarce profiles (principal, partner, technical architects).

### 3.5 AI agent economics

People agents: 1 / 3 / 6 at year-end (COMPANY.md §6.1). Average in-year agents are modeled as 0.5 / 2.0 / 4.5. Fully loaded cost is £27k per agent-year, inside the COMPANY.md §5.2 range of £20k to £35k.

| | FY2026 | FY2027 | FY2028 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Average agents | 0.5 | 2.0 | 4.5 |
| Notional fully loaded cost (£k) at £27k | 13.5 | 54.0 | 121.5 |
| Metered usage booked in People budget (£k) | 3 | 10 | 48 |
| Balance carried in central AI compute and tooling (£k, memo only) | 10.5 | 44.0 | 73.5 |
| Admin hours released per agent-year (assumed) | 600 | 600 | 600 |
| Total hours released | 300 | 1,200 | 2,700 |
| Equivalent human FTE at 1,600 productive hours | 0.2 | 0.8 | 1.7 |
| Cost per hour released at notional cost (£) | 45 | 45 | 45 |
| Loaded human cost per hour of a Consultant-level specialist (£) = £58k x 1.28 / 1,600 | 46 | 46 | 46 |

Platform, licence and model costs for the department's agents sit mainly in the central AI compute and tooling lines (COMPANY.md §5.2) so the People budget carries metered usage only. The cost-per-hour parity shown above is deliberate: agents earn their place through 24x7 availability, speed (candidate screening summaries and onboarding checklist chasing in minutes), and consistency, not through a lower hourly cost. Agents never make final hiring, disciplinary, pay or dismissal decisions: a named human owner reviews each (COMPANY.md §9.4), and for candidate screening the UK GDPR Article 22 and ICO guidance on automated decision-making apply.

## 4. Assumptions

1. Loaded cost = base salary x 1.28 for employer NI, pension, benefits and bonus accrual (COMPANY.md §5.2, §8.5). All base salaries are inside the §6.2 band for the stated level; the CPO base of £145,000 is inside the head band of £140,000 to £200,000.
2. Average FTE per role is the fraction of the year in seat from the blueprint.md section 5 start dates (for example People Operations Specialist from 2026-09-01 = 0.33 of FY2026; Talent Acquisition Partner from 2027-04-01 = 0.75 of FY2027). Planning bases are those in blueprint.md section 3.
3. The opening headcount at 1 January 2026 is treated as zero. Attrition (14% / 12.5% / 11%) is applied to average headcount; FY2027 is the midpoint of the 14% to 11% range in COMPANY.md §8.3. All leavers are backfilled.
4. Time-to-hire is 45 days for humans and agent provisioning is 5 days (COMPANY.md §8.3). The revenue-at-risk figures in 3.3 are an upper bound because only some vacancies are billable delivery seats.
5. Agency fee percentages (15% / 18% / 20%) and referral bonuses (£2.0k / £2.0k / £2.5k, paid after six-month probation) are modeled assumptions typical of UK professional-services hiring. Agency terms usually include a 3-month rebate; the model assumes 3% of agency spend is rebated and nets this against the fee figures, so none is shown separately.
6. Payroll is run through an external provider and HRIS; the People department performs payroll liaison and approvals only. Payroll provider fees are inside the HRIS and payroll platform line, priced per employee per month. HMRC filings are submitted by the provider with Finance sign-off.
7. UK employment basics are assumed in costing: right-to-work checks before day one, statutory sick pay, holiday accrual of 28 days including bank holidays pro rata, auto-enrolment pension at the employer contribution included in the 28% on-cost, written statement of employment particulars from day one. The employment-law retainer covers disciplinary, grievance and settlement-agreement advice.
8. US hires carry a +15% salary premium (COMPANY.md §6.2) and sit in FY2028 only after the ARR and ISO 27001 gates (§8.6). People headcount for US employees is not increased; an employer-of-record provider is assumed and its cost is included in the relocation, visa and US onboarding line.
9. AI model and compute unit costs fall about 15% a year (COMPANY.md §8.4); the metered usage line already reflects that decline against rising volume.
10. No bonus, equity or salary-review budget for the wider company is held in People: those costs sit in each department's loaded rate (bonus accrual) and in Executive (equity governance). The People department carries only its own team cost plus the programmes in section 1.
11. Inflation of 4% a year on base salaries is not applied to People's own bands because the §5.4 envelope is fixed; band-level inflation is covered in the sensitivities (section 5.1).
