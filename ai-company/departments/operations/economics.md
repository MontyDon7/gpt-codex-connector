## 4. Assumptions

1. Loaded cost is base x 1.28 (COMPANY.md §5.2). Planning bases are flat across years and inside the §6.2 bands; none is a US hire.
2. Average FTE reflects start quarters in section 2; humans at year end are 2 / 6 / 12 (§6.1). The COO is in post from Day 0 (2026-01-05).
3. The §5.4 envelope is binding; non-people lines were sized to the envelope, not the reverse. In particular, the London workspace line (£30k / £62k / £190k) is the modeled operations share of workplace cost on a client-site-first working model, and company-wide premises, insurance and professional fees in COMPANY.md §5.2 ("premises, T&E, insurance, professional fees, other", £1.1m / £2.7m / £5.8m) are assumed to be spread across departments' non-people lines rather than held entirely by Operations.
4. The allocation of department cost to services in section 3 (30 / 15 / 20 / 15 / 15 / 5) is a management allocation for unit economics only.
5. Addressable share of company non-people spend is 75%; competed share rises from 45% to 60% as the RFQ process matures; savings rates are 5% / 6% / 7% of competed spend measured against the first compliant quote.
6. Purchase orders are 90 / 260 / 620 (about one PO per £29k, £25k and £23k of addressable spend respectively, including many small recurring orders). Order-size mix: 55% below £2,500 in FY2026 and FY2027 and 50% in FY2028; formal tenders are 4 / 12 / 25.
7. Active vendors: 45 / 110 / 220 at year end; new vendor reviews: 30 / 70 / 130 per year; critical vendors 6 / 14 / 28.
8. Working model: consultants work mainly at client premises; HQ and New York provide team days, project rooms and client meetings. Flexible-space memberships and a serviced office in New York (short break clause) are used before any long lease; no long lease is committed in FY2026 or FY2027.
9. Compliance actions opened: 24 / 60 / 110 per year, driven by regulatory changes, Legal findings, certification surveillance (ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus in FY2026, SOC 2 Type II in FY2027) and vendor-related findings.
10. Administrative hours released per average agent-year: 500, valued at £45 per hour notional cost, consistent with the `people` economics.
11. Agent costs are fully loaded (compute, model usage, licences, monitoring); unit compute cost falls about 15% a year (COMPANY.md §8 item 4) but volume per agent rises, so average cost per agent is nearly flat at about £21k (within the §5.2 £20k to £35k range). Each agent has a named human owner (§9.4).
12. The contingency line (FY2028, £30k) is COO-held, not present in earlier years, and releases only inside the non-budgeted spend authority of COMPANY.md §9.3 (above £50k CFO, above £250k CEO).
13. Gross hires (42 / 81 / 139) are the People department forecast; leavers use attrition of 14% / 14% / 11% (COMPANY.md §8, assumption 3).
14. USD spend for New York is converted at the fixed planning rate of £1 = $1.27 (COMPANY.md §1); the FY2027 New York line of £50k is about $63.5k and FY2028 £265k about $336.6k.
