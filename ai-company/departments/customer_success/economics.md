## 3. Pricing and unit economics

Customer Success does not sell a priced product. The Keep retainer, whose tiers and SLAs it administers, is priced in COMPANY.md §3.3. This section shows the cost to serve against that price and the retention economics that justify the department.

### 3.1 Keep tiers and service commitments (COMPANY.md §3.3)

| Tier | Price | Annual value | Support days a quarter | Named client manager | Cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep Essential | £8,000 / month | £96,000 | 5 | Pooled | Business hours (08:00 to 18:00 UK) |
| Keep Plus | £18,000 / month | £216,000 | 15 | Yes | Business hours |
| Keep Enterprise | £40,000 / month | £480,000 | Embedded team | Yes, plus executive steering committee | 24x7 for P1 and P2 |

Keep is invoiced monthly in advance on a 12-month minimum term with an annual uplift of CPI + 2%, capped at 7% (COMPANY.md §7). Planning CPI is 3.0%, giving a 5.0% uplift: Keep Plus renews at £216,000 x 1.05 = £226,800 a year (the figure used in the renewal scenario), Essential at £100,800, Enterprise at £504,000.

SLA targets, in hours (first response / resolution or workaround) by tier and priority:

| Priority | Keep Essential | Keep Plus | Keep Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 service down, data exposed or wrong actions in production | 4 / 10 | 1 / 4 | 0.25 / 4 (24x7) |
| P2 major degradation | 10 / 30 | 4 / 20 | 1 / 8 (24x7) |
| P3 single-user or workaround exists | 20 / 50 | 10 / 30 | 4 / 20 |
| P4 question or minor request | 30 / none | 20 / none | 10 / none |

Hours are business hours (a business day is 10 hours, 08:00 to 18:00 UK) except Enterprise P1 and P2, which run 24x7. These are department assumptions, defined once in sops.md (SOP-CUSTOMER_SUCCESS-03) and mirrored in `agent.py`.

## 4. Assumptions

1. Planning CPI is 3.0%, so the renewal uplift is CPI + 2% = 5.0%, below the 7% cap (COMPANY.md §7). `agent.py` holds this as `CPI_PLANNING_PCT = 3.0` and `RENEWAL_UPLIFT_PCT = 5.0`.
2. The Chief Customer Officer starts in Q3 FY2026 (0.5 of a year at £140k base, band minimum, COMPANY.md §6.2). This is the only start date that fits the £90k FY2026 people envelope with one human at the head-of-department band.
3. Customer Success Manager base salaries are £92k (first hire) and £94k (later hires), Client Support Specialists £55k, Analysts £40k, Support Lead £95k, Renewals and Commercial Manager £100k; all inside the §6.2 bands.
4. A named client manager can carry up to 22 clients; the CCO counts as the first named manager for the largest accounts. This is `CLIENTS_PER_NAMED_CSM = 22` in `agent.py`.
5. Support days are drawn down by the Keep pod in Delivery. Customer Success bears the desk (front door) cost only, at 0.25 desk-day per support day used. A support day is 7.5 working hours of named-team effort. Average entitlement utilisation is 70%.
6. Keep SLA targets are those in section 3.1, chosen to be credible for a team of 2 to 5 desk staff and consistent with COMPANY.md §3.3 (24x7 incident cover for Enterprise only). SLAs are commitments to respond, not guarantees of resolution; any service credit is a commercial concession approved under COMPANY.md §9.3.
7. Retention planning rates are those of COMPANY.md §5.1: logo retention 83% (FY2027), 85% (FY2028); NRR of retained clients 115% and 118%. The FY2026 targets of 83% logo retention and a 100% NRR floor are planning values for the first cohort.
8. Cost of revenue is defined as delivery plus customer_success cost (COMPANY.md §5.1). Customer Success cost is not recharged to clients.
9. No US-based Customer Success hires are planned; US hours are covered by a late UK shift. The USD planning rate is £1 = $1.27 (COMPANY.md §1) for any US client events spend.
10. Agent unit costs of £25k, £22k and £20k per agent-year fall about 15% a year in model cost but are floored at the £20k lower bound of COMPANY.md §5.2 for tooling and integration.
11. Amounts are ex-VAT. Employer on-costs of 28% are within the loaded cost.
