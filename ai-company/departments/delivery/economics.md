### 3.6 Billing and cash mechanics for fixed-fee work

Milestones 30% on signature, 40% at mid-point, 30% on acceptance; invoices issued within 2 business days of milestone acceptance; net 30 (`COMPANY.md §7`).

Delivery cost is incurred roughly evenly across the five months, so each Forge Build is cash-negative to Delivery for about 1.5 months before the mid-point cash arrives. Delivery obligations: submit milestone acceptance evidence to Finance within 1 business day (`delivery.milestone_reached`), keep acceptance disputes below 5% of milestones, and hold DSO at or below 45 days with bad debt below 1% of revenue (Finance-owned targets, Delivery-influenced via acceptance quality).

## 4. Assumptions

1. 225 available working days per FTE per year (260 less 25 days leave, 8 bank holidays, 2 training).
2. Utilisation targets per `COMPANY.md §4`; Senior Consultant 75% and head 20% are assumptions derived so the blended figure stays at 70%.
3. Average FTE is below year-end FTE because hiring runs through the year; average agents 4.0 / 14.0 / 37.0 against year-end 6 / 22 / 52.
4. Loaded cost = base x 1.28 (`COMPANY.md §6.2`, §8). Base salaries stay inside the §6.2 bands; US hires +15%. About 12 New York hires in FY2028 at base +15% (same as `blueprint.md`; about 9 time-weighted FTE); a smaller number in FY2027 is absorbed in the FY2027 average base.
5. Agent cost per agent-year £30k / £27k / £24k inside the £20k to £35k range; model and compute unit cost falls about 15% a year (`COMPANY.md §8`).
6. Subcontractor and associate day cost £900 blended; subcontractors used for peaks and niche skills, capped by the policy ceiling in section 1.
7. Recruitment fees, onboarding, laptops and employer-brand cost sit in the `people` and `it_security` budgets, not here. Delivery bears only the productivity cost of vacancy and ramp (section 6).
8. Attrition 14% (FY2026) falling to 11% (FY2028) (`COMPANY.md §8`); time-to-hire 45 days; vacancy plus ramp costs about 52 billable-day equivalents per leaver (about 32 working days of vacancy and 20 days of lost productivity during ramp).
9. Unrecharged T&E is the share of travel not billable under the 5% expense cap, plus internal travel and the FY2027 New York launch.
10. Training, certification, methodology and external QA covers practitioner certifications, an external quality-review panel, methodology build and the SOC 2 and ISO 27001 evidence work attributable to delivery.
11. The department's FY2026 to FY2028 revenue mix and tier prices follow `COMPANY.md §3` and §5.1. Tier-level cost ratios in 3.4 are indicative; only the line-level ratios in 3.3 reconcile to the envelope.
12. Keep ARR tier mix about 65/35/0 (Essential / Plus / Enterprise) in FY2026, 45/45/10 in FY2027, 30/45/25 in FY2028.
13. All delivery effort is quality-gated: every client deliverable is reviewed by a Manager or above before release, and agents never release deliverables without human QA (`COMPANY.md §4`, §9.4). The Manager review time is inside Manager utilisation.

## 7. Risks to the economics

1. **Yield (largest).** Section 3.7 shows the revenue plan needs about 2.3x to 2.6x rate-card yield on effort. If Forge and Keep are sold closer to effort-based prices, FY2028 revenue falls by up to about £10m at the same effort, and delivery margin falls with it.
2. **Fixed-fee overrun.** The fixed-fee model puts overrun risk on Delivery; every 5% overrun costs £101k / £228k / £459k. Control: scope baselines at signature, a change request raised the day scope moves, and a Manager sign-off on any engagement forecast above 105% of baseline effort.
3. **Agent leverage slower than planned.** If agents deliver less of the research and drafting load, the 0.5 to 0.65 agents per delivery human (`COMPANY.md §2`) is not achieved and human days rise; the lever is hiring more Consultants than the budget allows.
4. **Senior capacity.** Partner and Principal time is margin-negative on a billable basis (3.2); an under-resourced senior layer reduces quality-gate review capacity, and a delivery shortfall delays milestones and cash (3.6).
5. **Hiring pace.** The headcount curve (14 to 46 to 104) requires about 32 and 58 net hires in FY2027 and FY2028 plus replacements; a 45-day time-to-hire against a 5-day agent provisioning time pushes effort toward subcontractors and agents when hiring lags.
6. **Client concentration.** Policy ceiling of 15% of revenue per client (`COMPANY.md §8`): a delivery failure at the largest client has an outsized margin effect because the engagement team is small and cannot be redeployed quickly.
