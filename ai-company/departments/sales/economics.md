### 3.2 Why sales cost per proposal rises

Cost per proposal rises 53% from FY2026 to FY2028 (£10,625 to £16,254) while average deal value rises 32% (ACV £250k to £330k). Drivers: more senior-led pursuits (Forge Build and Scale, Compass Programme) need Solutions Consultant and Sales Director time; US pursuits add travel and counsel (£330k US programme in FY2028); pursuit investment rises from £5k to £250k as 25 large pursuits per year need funded workshops. Sales cost per £1 of new-logo bookings is 14.2p, 15.6p and 16.4p; the ratio is stable because cost grows with deal size.

|---|---|---|

Pricing guardrails (agreed with Finance, `finance.pricing_check_completed`): Finance applies the company-average cost ratio (1 minus gross margin) to list price and returns `max_discount_pct` where the deal would otherwise breach the margin floor. The table shows the margin after discount d, 1 minus (1 minus GM) / (1 minus d), and the discount at which the deal reaches the floor.

| Year | Company gross margin | Margin after 10% discount | Margin floor (GM minus 8 points) | Indicative discount at the floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2026 | 48% | 42.2% | 40% | 13.3% |
| FY2027 | 58% | 53.3% | 50% | 16.0% |
| FY2028 | 64% | 60.0% | 56% | 18.2% |

The first-order discount ceiling is therefore 10% (Sales head authority); a discount between 10% and the indicative floor is a Finance head and CEO decision; above the floor the deal needs CFO and CEO sign-off as a loss-leader. In crisis mode (`executive.crisis_declared`) the Sales ceiling for any proposal is 5% (sales policy, applied by the Deal Desk Agent) and a discount above 5% is not offered. If Finance returns `approved` false the deal is re-priced at list (cap 0%) and a re-pricing task is raised within 3 days.

Cost of discount: the average discount target is 6.0%, so list-equivalent bookings are £6.38m / £14.36m / £29.84m against £6.0m / £13.5m / £28.05m net, a leakage of £0.38m / £0.86m / £1.79m. Each 1 point of average discount removes £60k / £135k / £281k of new-logo bookings.

### 3.8 Renewal pricing (Keep)

Annual uplift is CPI + 2%, capped at 7% (COMPANY.md §7). The planning CPI is 3.0% (assumption), so the default uplift is 5.0%. A client that is at risk (health score below 60 or a churn-risk flag open) receives a save offer: the uplift is waived (0%), and any additional discount stays inside the 10% Sales head authority; above that needs the Finance head and CEO.

| Tier | Annual value | Renewal at 5.0% (CPI 3.0%) | Renewal at 7% cap | Save offer (0% uplift) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep Essential | £96,000 | £100,800 | £102,720 | £96,000 |
| Keep Plus | £216,000 | £226,800 | £231,120 | £216,000 |
| Keep Enterprise | £480,000 | £504,000 | £513,600 | £480,000 |

Renewal proposals are issued at least 60 days before term end. Keep revenue at risk each year is the opening ARR of terms ending in the year (FY2026 closing ARR £1.5m means FY2027 renewals worth about £1.5m before uplift; FY2028 about £6.5m); a one-point miss on renewal retention is therefore about £15k and £65k of ARR respectively.

## 4. Assumptions

1. Budget lines and people cost are fitted to COMPANY.md §5.4; the split into lines, unit costs and cohort bases is a derivation by this department, not a company fact.
2. The founding sales team (Chief Revenue Officer, Senior Account Executive, Account Executive, Sales Development Representative) is in seat from simulation day 0 (hired with seed funding before the Series A); the Solutions Consultant joins in Q3 FY2026.
3. Role bases are by hire cohort and held flat after hire, except the Chief Revenue Officer (£185,000 from FY2027); the pay-review and true-up reserve absorbs the rounding (£2.2k, -£1.1k, -£3.0k). Annual merit above this would need a headcount-timing offset.
4. Recruitment fees (typically 15% to 20% of base for agency-sourced hires) are paid from the `people` department budget, not Sales.
5. Win rate 30%, lead-to-qualified 25%, qualified-to-proposal 50% apply to marketing-sourced and sales-sourced leads alike (COMPANY.md §5.3, §8 item 2). Planning split of the 25%: `mql` leads about 50%, `nurture` leads about 5%, with `mql` about 45% of leads.
6. ACV used for bookings equals the company ACV (COMPANY.md §5.1) and new-logo bookings are logos x ACV; the average discount of 6% is already inside that ACV (net).
7. Commission is a non-people cost at 1.7% of net contract value plus 15% employer NI; renewals and expansion in retained clients earn the same rate on first-year value (allowance sized from the 115% and 118% net revenue retention in COMPANY.md §5.1).
8. Agent cost in the sales budget is direct inference and tool seats only (about £5k to £8k per agent-year); the COMPANY.md §5.2 average of £20k to £35k per agent-year is fully loaded and includes shared platform, governance and oversight time carried by `data`, `it_security` and `product`.
9. US pursuits start Q1 FY2027 when the New York office opens; leads from US companies before then are parked. US activity is priced in USD at £1 = $1.27 (COMPANY.md §1).
10. Sales carries no cost of revenue; it is operating expense (COMPANY.md §5.1: only `delivery` and `customer_success` are cost of revenue). Pre-sales scoping time is delivered by Delivery principals and is recovered in Delivery's utilisation, not charged to Sales.
11. The decision window in section 3.7 is a planning parameter derived to hold the 75-day sales cycle; the first year of actual data replaces it.
12. Keep renewals begin in FY2027 because the 12-month minimum term (COMPANY.md §3.3) means no Keep term ends in FY2026.
