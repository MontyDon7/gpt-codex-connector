## 3. Pricing and unit economics

The Executive department sells nothing. Its economics are the cost of governance relative to the company it governs, and the cost and value of the decisions it gates.

### 3.1 Cost of the executive function

| Metric | FY2026 | FY2027 | FY2028 | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Department total cost (£k) | 600 | 1,050 | 1,300 | `COMPANY.md §5.4` |
| Company revenue (£k) | 6,000 | 19,500 | 46,200 | `COMPANY.md §5.1` |
| Executive cost as % of revenue | 10.0% | 5.4% | 2.8% | total / revenue |
| Executive cost per active client (£k) | 25.0 | 16.2 | 9.3 | total / 24, 65, 140 clients |
| Share of company total cost (£k basis) | 8.0% | 5.9% | 3.3% | total / 7,500, 17,800, 39,000 (`COMPANY.md §5.2`) |
| Revenue per executive-department human (year-end, £k) | 2,000 | 4,875 | 9,240 | revenue / 3, 4, 5 humans |

### 3.2 Governance unit costs

| Unit | FY2026 | FY2027 | FY2028 | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board meetings per year (4 quarterly plus 2 ad hoc, assumed) | 6 | 6 | 6 | Assumption A3 |
| Board and governance cost per meeting (£k) | 7.5 | 14.2 | 20.0 | Board and governance line / 6 |
| Deals of £500k or more reviewed (assumed) | 3 | 7 | 14 | Assumption A4: about 10% of new logos (24, 45, 85) plus upsells |
| Requisitions decided (assumed) | 42 | 81 | 139 | Net adds plus backfills, Assumption A5 |
| Deal decision-desk cost per deal (£k) | 4.6 | 2.7 | 1.4 | Chief of Staff loaded cost x 10% time / deals: 138.7 x 0.10 / 3; 189.4 x 0.10 / 7; 192.0 x 0.10 / 14 |
| Headcount decision-desk cost per requisition (£k) | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.1 | Chief of Staff loaded cost x 10% time / requisitions: 138.7 x 0.10 / 42; 189.4 x 0.10 / 81; 192.0 x 0.10 / 139 |

Even at the FY2026 figure, the desk cost of a £500k deal decision (£4.6k) is under 1% of the contract value.

### 3.3 Value at stake in gated decisions

| Gate | Exposure per decision | Control cost | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deal of £500k or more | £500k to £1.5m (CEO), above £1.5m (Board) | about £4.6k desk cost plus 48 hour SLA | Prevents off-margin deals (gross margin target 48% in FY2026, `COMPANY.md §5.1`) and client concentration above 15% of revenue |
| Headcount outside budget | Loaded cost of one hire, for example Manager £92k to £115k base, £118k to £147k loaded | 5 day SLA | Protects EBITDA of -£1.5m in FY2026 (`COMPANY.md §5.1`) |
| Crisis declaration | Breach fines (UK GDPR up to 4% of turnover in principle) and client trust; 72 hour ICO clock | 4 hour declaration SLA | Starts the mobilisation clock early |

Worked example for concentration: in FY2026 the 15% cap is 15% x £6.0m = £900k of revenue in a year from a single client. Calder Freight Group buying Forge Scale (£650k) and Keep Plus (£216k a year) in the same year is £866k, which is 14.4% of plan and under the cap, but leaves only £34k of headroom. The deal decision records that headroom as a condition.

## 4. Assumptions

| ID | Assumption |
|---|---|
| A1 | Loaded cost per human = base x 1.28 (`COMPANY.md §5.2`). Agent cost £20k to £35k per agent-year; FY2026 £22k, FY2027 average £26k to £27k, FY2028 £30k, consistent with compute unit costs falling about 15% a year (`COMPANY.md §8`, assumption 4) offset by agent scope growth. |
| A2 | Independent director fees (Chair, Audit and Risk NED) are fictional modeled assumptions. Investor directors are unpaid. |
| A3 | Board cadence is 4 quarterly meetings plus 2 ad hoc meetings a year (`COMPANY.md §9.1` requires quarterly plus ad hoc). |
| A4 | Deals of £500k or more: 3, 7 and 14 in FY2026 to FY2028 (about 10% of new logos plus expansion deals). The Chief of Staff spends 10% of time on deal decisions. |
| A5 | Requisitions decided = net adds plus backfills. Net adds are 38 (FY2026, treating the opening team as Day 0 hires), 72 and 120 (`COMPANY.md §6`: 38 to 110 to 230); backfills use attrition of 14%, 12.5% (interpolated) and 11% (`COMPANY.md §8`, assumption 3) on average humans of 29, 74, 170: about 4, 9 and 19. Totals 42, 81 and 139. The Chief of Staff spends 10% of time on headcount. |
| A6 | Executive-search fee 25% of first-year base for principal-level and above, and for manager hires in this department; other hires use the People pipeline. |
| A7 | Discount ceiling for CEO approval is 15%; above 15% the deal is declined and returned to Sales. `COMPANY.md §9.3` requires Finance head and CEO above 10% and sets no upper ceiling, so 15% is an executive policy assumption. |
| A8 | Minimum deal gross margin for approval is 35% against a company gross margin target of 48% in FY2026 (`COMPANY.md §5.1`). Policy assumption used by the agent. |
| A9 | Hiring hold triggered at 15% department budget variance; liquidity crisis at runway below 6 months. Policy assumptions. |
| A10 | All figures in GBP, ex-VAT. US spend at £1 = $1.27 (`COMPANY.md §1`). |
