## 3. Pricing and unit economics

Finance does not sell; its economics are the cost to bill and collect, the cash cycle, and guardrails that protect client-contract margin.

### 3.1 Order-to-cash unit economics

Order-to-cash (O2C) cost is allocated at 30% of department cost (billing, collections, cash application, credit control, revenue-recognition support).

| Metric | FY2026 | FY2027 | FY2028 |
|---|---|---|---|
| O2C cost allocation, £k (30% of department total) | 93 | 219 | 450 |
| Invoices issued (assumption, section 4) | 140 | 520 | 1,350 |
| Cost per invoice issued and collected, £ | 664 | 421 | 333 |
| O2C cost per £ of revenue | 1.55p | 1.12p | 0.97p |
| Month-end close, working days (target) | 8 | 6 | 5 |

Invoice volume driver: fixed-fee Compass and Forge invoice at three milestones (30% on signature, 40% mid-point, 30% on acceptance) and Keep is invoiced monthly in advance (COMPANY.md §7).

### 3.2 Cash cycle and working capital

DSO target is 45 days or less and bad debt below 1% of revenue (COMPANY.md §7). Receivables are modeled on ex-VAT revenue; UK invoices add 20% VAT, which is collected and passed to HMRC.

| Revenue, £k (COMPANY.md §5.1) | 6,000 | 19,500 | 46,200 |
| Revenue per day, £k (revenue / 365) | 16.4 | 53.4 | 126.6 |
| Receivables at DSO 45, £k (ex-VAT) | 740 | 2,404 | 5,696 |
| Value of one DSO day, £k | 16.4 | 53.4 | 126.6 |

| Bad-debt ceiling at 1% of revenue, £k | 60 | 195 | 462 |

Collections ladder (COMPANY.md §7): reminder day 31, call day 38, finance-head escalation day 45, legal letter day 60. Statutory late-payment interest is 8% over base rate. Cost-of-capital rate of 8% is an assumption (section 4).

### 3.3 Cash profile of a fixed-fee engagement (illustration)

Forge Build list price £260,000 (COMPANY.md §3.2), milestone billing 30/40/30, net 30, expenses capped at 5% of fee (§7).

| Event | Invoice (ex-VAT, £) | VAT 20% (£) | Cash in at net 30 (£, incl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signature (day 0) | 78,000 | 15,600 | 93,600 on day 30 |
| Mid-point milestone (about month 3) | 104,000 | 20,800 | 124,800 on about day 120 |
| Acceptance (about month 5) | 78,000 | 15,600 | 93,600 on about day 180 |

Delivery cost is incurred ahead of the mid-point and acceptance receipts, so late milestone acceptance by the client delays the final 30% (£78,000 plus VAT) and is the main driver of the DSO sensitivity in section 3.5.

### 3.4 Pricing and discount guardrails (pricing checks)

Gross margin is (revenue minus cost of revenue) / revenue with cost of revenue equal to the delivery and customer_success departments (COMPANY.md §5.1). The table applies the company average cost ratio (1 minus gross margin) to list price and shows the margin if the maximum sales-head discount of 10% (COMPANY.md §9.3) is given with no change in cost to serve.

| Tier | List price (£) | Implied cost at 58% GM (FY2027, £) | Price at 10% discount (£) | Margin after 10% discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compass Spark | 45,000 | 18,900 | 40,500 | 53.3% |
| Compass Sprint | 120,000 | 50,400 | 108,000 | 53.3% |
| Compass Programme | 350,000 | 147,000 | 315,000 | 53.3% |
| Forge Pilot | 90,000 | 37,800 | 81,000 | 53.3% |
| Forge Build | 260,000 | 109,200 | 234,000 | 53.3% |
| Forge Scale | 650,000 | 273,000 | 585,000 | 53.3% |

Equivalent post-discount margins at company-average cost ratios are 42.2% (FY2026, GM 48%) and 60.0% (FY2028, GM 64%). Every 1 point of average discount removes 1% of revenue and, with cost to serve unchanged, the same amount of EBITDA: £195k in FY2027 (about 11% of the £1.7m EBITDA target) and £462k in FY2028.

Pricing check rules run by finance on `sales.deal_approval_requested` and quote reviews:

| Check | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Discount band | ≤10%: Sales head approves; >10% or non-standard terms: Finance head and CEO (COMPANY.md §9.3) | Finance flags, does not approve alone |
| Margin floor | Deal margin below company-average GM minus 8 points for the year (40% FY2026, 50% FY2027, 56% FY2028) | Escalate to CFO with change-order options |
| Payment terms | Standard is net 30; anything beyond net 45 or 100% in arrears | Treated as non-standard; CFO and CEO |
| Concentration | Client share of revenue above 15% (COMPANY.md §8 item 8) | Flag to CEO and Board Audit and Risk Committee |
| Deal size | ≥ £500k to CEO; > £1.5m Board (COMPANY.md §9.3) | Finance supplies cash and margin pack |
| Rate card | Change orders at §3.5 rates; agent-day £350 | Block billing at non-rate-card values |
| USD quotes | Quoted at £1 = $1.27 fixed planning rate | FX variance reported monthly |

## 4. Assumptions

1. Loaded cost is base x 1.28 (COMPANY.md §5.2); FY2028 US Finance Lead base includes +15% (§6.2). Base salaries rise about 3% a year for continuing roles (CFO 140 / 150 / 155; Controller 96 / 98 / 105).
2. Average FTE reflects start quarters in section 2; humans at year end are 2 / 5 / 10 (§6.1). The FY2026 average (1.25) reflects a Q4 Financial Controller hire; the CFO is in post from day 0.
3. Invoice counts (140 / 520 / 1,350) derive from COMPANY.md §5.1 and §7: about 24 / 65 / 140 active clients, three milestone invoices per fixed-fee project, 12 invoices per Keep client-year, and Keep clients rising with ARR (£1.5m / £6.5m / £17.0m). They are volume assumptions, not forecasts.
4. O2C allocation of 30% of department cost is a management allocation, used for unit economics only; it does not change the budget.
5. Cost of capital for carrying receivables is 8%, used only for working-capital sensitivities; it is not a financing assumption for the Series A or seed funds (COMPANY.md §1).
6. Agent costs are fully loaded (compute, model usage, licences, monitoring) and fall about 15% a year (§8 item 4); each agent has a named human owner (§9.4).
7. External audit begins with FY2026 statutory accounts; Lumenhall is a small company by Companies House thresholds early on but the investors require an audit, so the fee is included.
8. USD revenue is 0% / 15% / 30% (§1); quoted at £1 = $1.27 fixed planning rate.
9. Margin checks in section 3.4 apply the company-average cost ratio to list price; real engagements vary by staffing pyramid (§4) and utilisation (70% target).
10. The contingency line (FY2028, £30k) is CFO-held, is not in earlier years, and releases only through the non-budgeted spend authority in COMPANY.md §9.3 (> £50k CFO, > £250k CEO).
