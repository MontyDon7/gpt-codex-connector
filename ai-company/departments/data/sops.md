# Data and Analytics Standard Operating Procedures

Department: `data` (agent class `DataAgent`, the Metrics and KPI Reporting Agent). Head: Dr Emeka Nwosu, Chief Data and AI Officer (COMPANY.md §9.2). Scope: firm-wide metrics and reporting, data engineering, BI, forecasting, data governance and AI agent quality. UK context: UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 (ICO is the regulator), ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II baselines (COMPANY.md §8.7). Team in FY2026: 1 human and 1 AI agent (COMPANY.md §6.1), and the human joins on 2026-07-01 (`blueprint.md` §5). Until then Finance and Operations cover reporting oversight and the agent runs on the event stream. All figures are modelled assumptions, not real data.

Conventions used throughout:

- `[human]` steps are performed by the Chief Data and AI Officer unless a SOP names another blueprint role. Roles that do not exist yet in a given year are covered by the Chief Data and AI Officer (see `kpis.md` owners): Senior Data Engineer from FY2027 Q3, BI and Analytics Analyst from FY2027 Q4, and from FY2028 the Principal Data Architect, Data Engineering Manager, Senior Forecasting Scientist and Data Governance Lead. Until the Chief Data and AI Officer starts (2026-07-01) the Finance head and the Chief Operating Officer hold the `[human]` steps for month-end reporting and data quality respectively.
- `[agent]` steps are performed by the `data` agent working under the named accountable owner (`blueprint.md` §4). Agents may draft, analyse, monitor and propose; they may not sign contracts, release client deliverables without human QA, approve payments above £10,000, make hiring or termination decisions or notify regulators (COMPANY.md §9.4). The agent may not change a certified metric definition or alter source data.
- Money is GBP ex VAT (`amount_gbp`). Sim days are integers from Day 0 = 2026-01-05 (COMPANY.md §1); `due_day` keys are sim days. "Working day" (WD) is Monday to Friday; the simulation does not model UK bank holidays. Business IDs follow CONVENTIONS section 4.
- Reports, forecasts and issues carry sequence ids generated by the agent: `KR-0001` (KPI report), `FC-0001` (forecast), `DQ-0001` (data quality issue), `AQ-0001` (agent quality report), `BM-0001` (benchmark). Metric ids are the ones in `kpis.md` section 2.
- Client-data rule: contact and credential fields (`email`, `phone`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `address`, `password`, `token`, `api_key`) are stripped before any event body is logged or stored in a firm-wide mart (client-data segregation, COMPANY.md §9.4; `client_data_segregation_breaches` target 0).
- Every emitted message is addressed directly to each department the topic map in `core/topics.py` lists as a subscriber (one message per recipient, same body and same `*_id`); nothing is broadcast with `recipient` `*`. Consumers that count documents must count distinct `report_id`, `forecast_id`, `issue_id`, `benchmark_id` or `budget_cycle_id` values.

## SOP-DATA-01: Funnel and pipeline ingestion

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer, with the Metrics and KPI Reporting Agent computing the metrics.
**Trigger**: `marketing.lead_generated`, `marketing.campaign_launched`, `sales.lead_qualified`, `sales.proposal_sent`, `sales.deal_won`, `sales.deal_lost`
**Inputs**: Event bodies: `lead_id`, `deal_id`, `client_id`, `client_name`, `amount_gbp`, `service_line`, `annual_value_gbp` (Keep deals), campaign `budget_gbp`. Metric definitions in `kpis.md` section 2 (`leads_generated`, `leads_qualified`, `lead_to_qualified_rate`, `proposals_sent`, `open_pipeline_gbp`, `win_rate`, `bookings_gbp`).

### Steps

1. [agent] Run the SOP-DATA-08 checks on the event (completeness, validity, uniqueness) before using it. A failing check raises an issue but the event is still processed if it can be used safely.
2. [agent] Strip contact and credential fields from the body before logging; count fields removed.
3. [agent] Leads and qualified leads: count each `lead_id` once. A repeated `lead_id` is ignored and flagged as a uniqueness defect.
4. [agent] Proposals: store `deal_id` to `amount_gbp` in open pipeline (re-sent proposals replace the amount, and count once toward `proposals_sent`). Proposals without `deal_id` or with a negative or non-numeric amount are not added to pipeline.
5. [agent] Deal won: remove the deal from open pipeline, add the won amount to `bookings_gbp` (if the won amount is invalid, use the last quoted amount), record bookings by `service_line`, register the client as active. A duplicate `deal_id` is ignored. For a `keep` service line add `annual_value_gbp` (or the deal amount if absent) to `keep_arr_gbp`.
6. [agent] Deal lost: remove from open pipeline, count one decision and book the lost value. Duplicates ignored.
7. [agent] Campaigns: count the launch and add `budget_gbp` to campaign spend for the marketing-efficiency view.
8. [human] Weekly, the Chief Data and AI Officer spot-checks bookings and open pipeline against the Sales CRM (the system of record for deal stage, `kpis.md` section 3) and records any variance as a task.

### Outputs and messages

- No emitted message on a clean event. Defects emit `data.data_quality_issue_raised` (see SOP-DATA-08).
- State updates feed SOP-DATA-04 (KPI report), SOP-DATA-06 (forecast) and SOP-DATA-10 (benchmark win rate).

### SLA and controls

- Event processed the same sim day. Counts are idempotent: replays of a `lead_id`, `deal_id` or `invoice_id` never double count.
- Sales CRM wins any conflict on deal stage. Win rate is deals won over won plus lost (target 30%, COMPANY.md §5.3); lead to qualified target 25%.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Event with missing keys: processed if the remaining fields are safe; issue severity medium (high if money is missing).
- Pipeline and CRM variance above 5% of open pipeline: the Chief Data and AI Officer raises it with the Sales head within 1 working day.

## SOP-DATA-02: Delivery, billing and collections ingestion

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (Finance head covers the review until 2026-07-01).
**Trigger**: `delivery.engagement_kickoff`, `delivery.engagement_completed`, `finance.invoice_issued`, `finance.payment_received`
**Inputs**: `engagement_id`, `client_id`, `invoice_id`, `amount_gbp`, event sim day. Metric definitions `active_engagements`, `invoiced_gbp`, `collected_gbp`, `outstanding_receivables_gbp`, `dso_days`.

### Steps

1. [agent] Run SOP-DATA-08 checks (completeness; uniqueness of `engagement_id` and `invoice_id`; non-negative `amount_gbp`).
2. [agent] Kickoff: add the engagement to the active set once. Completion: remove it and count it. Active engagements = kickoffs minus completions.
3. [agent] Invoice issued: record `invoice_id`, amount and issue day; add to `invoiced_gbp`. Invalid, negative or repeated invoices are not added.
4. [agent] Payment received: add the amount to `collected_gbp`; if the `invoice_id` matches a known invoice, record DSO sample = payment day minus invoice day. Outstanding receivables = invoiced minus collected.
5. [human] Monthly, before SOP-DATA-04, the Chief Data and AI Officer reconciles `invoiced_gbp` and `collected_gbp` to the Finance ledger. The ledger is the source of truth for money (`kpis.md` section 3); differences go to Finance as a task.

### Outputs and messages

- No emitted message on a clean event; defects emit `data.data_quality_issue_raised`.
- Feeds the monthly report (`invoiced_gbp`, `collected_gbp`, `outstanding_receivables_gbp`, `dso_days`) and the forecast message (`active_engagements`, `outstanding_receivables_gbp`).

### SLA and controls

- Same sim day. DSO target at or below 45 days (COMPANY.md §7). Recognised revenue is never computed here: it comes from `finance.month_end_closed` (SOP-DATA-04) and Finance's figure is never overwritten.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Payment with no matching invoice: collected is still counted, no DSO sample, and a medium issue is raised if `invoice_id` was missing. Persistent unmatched cash goes to the Finance head.
- Engagement completion for an unknown `engagement_id`: counted as completed, flagged to Delivery through the issue process.

## SOP-DATA-03: Client health, renewals, churn and Keep ARR

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer.
**Trigger**: `customer_success.health_score_updated`, `customer_success.renewal_confirmed`, `customer_success.client_churned`
**Inputs**: `client_id`, `score`, `annual_value_gbp` or `amount_gbp`, `client_name`. Metrics `client_health_avg`, `active_clients`, `churned_clients`, `keep_arr_gbp`.

### Steps

1. [agent] Run SOP-DATA-08 checks (required `client_id`, and `score` on health events, which must be a number from 0 to 100).
2. [agent] Health: keep the latest numeric `score` per client; `client_health_avg` is the mean of those latest scores.
3. [agent] Renewal confirmed: count it, mark the client active, add `annual_value_gbp` to `keep_arr_gbp`.
4. [agent] Churn: count it, mark the client churned, drop the health score, subtract the churned annual value from `keep_arr_gbp` (never below zero).
5. [human] Monthly, the Chief Data and AI Officer reconciles active clients and Keep ARR to the Customer Success platform (source of truth for health) and Finance billing, ahead of the KPI report. Retention figures (net revenue retention 115% and 118%, logo retention 83% and 85%, COMPANY.md §5.1) are built from these counts.

### Outputs and messages

- No emitted message; state feeds SOP-DATA-04 and SOP-DATA-10. Defects emit `data.data_quality_issue_raised`.

### SLA and controls

- Churn is reflected in Keep ARR and active clients the same sim day. Keep ARR FY2026 target £1.5m (COMPANY.md §5.1).

### Exceptions and escalation

- Churn without a value: client status is updated and ARR is not changed; the Chief Data and AI Officer asks Customer Success for the value (human follow-up, not automated).
- A non-numeric or missing health score: not stored, and a completeness issue is raised (a score outside 0 to 100 is also not stored and raises a medium validity issue; the owner reviews outliers monthly).

## SOP-DATA-04: Monthly firm KPI report

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer, with the Metrics and KPI Reporting Agent drafting.
**Trigger**: Schedule: first day of each month (report for the month just ended), and `finance.month_end_closed`
**Inputs**: Metric snapshot from SOP-DATA-01 to SOP-DATA-03, KPI actuals for the department, recognised revenue from the month-end close (`revenue_gbp`, `period`).

### Steps

1. [agent] On the first tick of each month (normally the 1st; any later date if the clock skipped it), if events were seen in or before the previous month, build the report for the previous calendar month (`period` `YYYY-MM`).
2. [agent] Compute the metric snapshot and the department KPI actuals (report on-time rate, data quality pass rate, forecast MAPE once 4 forecasts are scored, agent evaluation coverage, segregation breaches).
3. [agent] On `finance.month_end_closed`, store `revenue_gbp` as recognised revenue and publish the report for the closed `period` if one has not been published yet.
4. [agent] Draft the narrative, never publishing a period twice. Measure timeliness: a monthly report (`monthly_schedule` or `month_end_closed`) published after working day 3 of the month following its period is late: it is counted, logged and a task is created for the Chief Data and AI Officer to tell the CEO and Finance head.
5. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer reviews the narrative and any variance flagged against the ledger. Figures going into the board pack or outside the firm need human sign-off (`blueprint.md` §4).
6. [agent] Emit the report.

### Outputs and messages

- `data.kpi_report_published`: `report_id`, `period`, `reason` (`monthly_schedule` | `month_end_closed` | `board_pack` | `crisis_declared` | `crisis_weekly` | `crisis_closed`), `metrics` (the full snapshot), `kpis` (name, target, actual), `sources`, `narrative`, `summary`. Audience: Executive and Finance (one addressed message each).

### SLA and controls

- Published by WD3 of the month after the period (`kpi_report_on_time_rate` = monthly reports on time / monthly reports published; board and crisis snapshots are not counted; target 95% in FY2026). One report per period; a month-end close publishes the matching period report instead of a second one.
- Certified metric ids are named in every report; certification is owned by SOP-DATA-12.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Late report: the Chief Data and AI Officer tells the CEO and Finance head with the cause and a revised date.
- Ledger disagreement on money: publish the ledger figure and note the variance; never overwrite Finance's recognised revenue.

## SOP-DATA-05: Board pack, OKRs, strategy and crisis reporting

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer.
**Trigger**: `executive.board_meeting_held`, `executive.okrs_set`, `executive.strategy_updated`, `executive.crisis_declared`
**Inputs**: Meeting day, OKR set, strategy change summary, crisis declaration; the current metric snapshot and certified marts.

### Steps

1. [agent] Board meeting held: publish a board-pack KPI snapshot for that meeting day.
2. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer signs off the board pack figures before they leave the firm (`kpis.md` section 3).
3. [agent] OKRs set: store the OKRs and create a task (10 days) to map each OKR to a certified metric and update registry targets.
4. [agent] Strategy updated: flag the review as pending and create a task (20 days) to review metric definitions and dashboards against the strategy.
5. [agent] Crisis declared: switch to crisis mode, create a critical task (1 day) for weekly KPI snapshots and daily cash and pipeline checks, and publish an immediate report.
6. [agent] While crisis mode is on, publish a KPI snapshot every 7 days (`crisis-day-<n>` period) until the crisis task is closed or the 90-day limit is reached.
7. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer reviews the crisis snapshots and tells Finance and the CEO which numbers are provisional.

### Outputs and messages

- `data.kpi_report_published` with `reason` `board_pack`, `crisis_declared`, `crisis_weekly` or `crisis_closed` (body as in SOP-DATA-04).
- Tasks: OKR mapping (10 days), strategy review (20 days), crisis monitoring (critical, 1 day).

### SLA and controls

- Board snapshot on the day the meeting is recorded; crisis report the same day, then every 7 days.
- A certified metric definition changes only through the registry process in SOP-DATA-12, not because an OKR asks for it.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Crisis snapshots that contradict Finance's liquidity view: escalate to the Finance head and the CEO the same day.
- No `executive.crisis_cleared` topic exists in the contract, so crisis reporting ends by a data-side rule: the Chief Data and AI Officer marks the crisis monitoring task done (or cancelled), or 90 days pass from the latest declaration, in which case a task is raised to confirm with the CEO and Finance head that the crisis is over or to re-declare. Either way the agent logs the close and publishes one final `crisis_closed` snapshot. A new `executive.crisis_declared` after a close starts a new crisis and task.

## SOP-DATA-06: Forecast production and error tracking

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (Senior Forecasting Scientist from FY2028, owner of the Forecasting Agent).
**Trigger**: Schedule: first day of each month, and `finance.cash_forecast_updated`
**Inputs**: Open pipeline, deals won and lost, bookings ledger by day, active engagements, outstanding receivables, Finance's cash forecast body (stored for reference; contact details stripped).

### Steps

1. [agent] At most one forecast per calendar month: whichever of the monthly schedule and `finance.cash_forecast_updated` arrives first publishes it.
2. [agent] Score earlier forecasts whose 90-day window has fully elapsed: APE = abs(actual bookings in window minus forecast) / actual bookings. Windows with zero actual bookings are not scored.
3. [agent] Win rate: the planning rate 30% until 5 won or lost decisions have been seen, then the observed win rate.
4. [agent] Forecast 90-day bookings = open pipeline x win rate. Band = the larger of 15% and the current MAPE. Expected new engagements = open deals x win rate.
5. [agent] Revenue forecast (90 days) = Keep ARR x 90/365 plus 40% of forecast bookings (`keep_revenue_gbp`, `project_revenue_gbp`, `forecast_revenue_gbp`, with the same band). Cash inflow forecast = outstanding receivables, all expected inside 90 days because the DSO target is 45 days (`forecast_cash_inflow_gbp`, never below zero). Capacity forecast: demand FTE = (active engagements + expected new engagements) x 3.0 FTE, required headcount = demand FTE / 70% utilisation (`capacity_demand_fte`, `capacity_required_headcount`). Only the bookings forecast is scored for MAPE.
6. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer (or the Senior Forecasting Scientist) approves any change of model, assumption or manual override, and the override is logged. The agent may not change the method on its own.
7. [agent] Emit the forecast, addressed to each subscriber.

### Outputs and messages

- `data.forecast_updated`: `forecast_id`, `reason`, `horizon_days` (90), `open_pipeline_gbp`, `win_rate_used`, `basis` (`observed` | `planning`), `forecast_bookings_gbp`, `low_gbp`, `high_gbp`, `mape_pct`, `expected_new_engagements`, `active_engagements`, `outstanding_receivables_gbp`, `forecast_revenue_gbp` (with `_low_gbp`, `_high_gbp`, `keep_revenue_gbp`, `project_revenue_gbp`), `forecast_cash_inflow_gbp`, `capacity_demand_fte`, `capacity_required_headcount`, `summary`. Audience: Sales, Finance, Delivery, Executive.

### SLA and controls

- `forecast_mape_bookings` is scored only after 4 forecasts; FY2026 target 15%, FY2027 10%, FY2028 8%. Every forecast carries a confidence band and the MAPE is published with it.
- Finance consumes `data.forecast_updated` as an input to the 13-week cash forecast, and Delivery uses `active_engagements` and expected new engagements as a demand signal.

### Exceptions and escalation

- MAPE above target for two consecutive months: the Chief Data and AI Officer reviews the method with the Sales head and the Finance head.
- Empty pipeline: forecast is zero with the band shown; it is still published so consumers do not use a stale figure.

## SOP-DATA-08: Data quality checks and issue resolution

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (Senior Data Engineer, owner of the Data Quality Monitor Agent, from FY2027 Q3).
**Trigger**: Schedule: every ingested event is validated on arrival (completeness, validity, uniqueness) and a weekly digest is produced on Monday
**Inputs**: The ingested message (`topic`, `sender`, body keys), required-key table per topic, unique-key table, `amount_gbp`.

### Steps

1. [agent] Completeness: for each ingested topic that has required keys (for example `sales.deal_won` needs `deal_id`, `client_id` and `amount_gbp`; `finance.invoice_issued` needs `invoice_id` and `amount_gbp`) check each is present and not blank.
2. [agent] Validity: any `amount_gbp` must be a non-negative number (booleans and strings fail).
3. [agent] Uniqueness: `deal_id` on `sales.deal_won`, `invoice_id` on `finance.invoice_issued`, `engagement_id` on `delivery.engagement_kickoff` must not repeat.
4. [agent] Severity: high for validity failures and for completeness failures on money fields or on any `finance.` topic; medium otherwise. Critical and low are set by a human (outages, cosmetic).
5. [agent] Raise one issue per topic, check type and defect per calendar month; suppress repeats and count them.
6. [human] The owner triages each issue, assigns it to the source department's owner and records the fix; the issue is closed when the corrected event or a documented waiver arrives.
7. [agent] Weekly: compute pass rate = checks passed / checks run and the age of open issues.
8. [agent] May quarantine a bad record in the staging layer; it may not delete source data or alter certified marts.

### Outputs and messages

- `data.data_quality_issue_raised`: `issue_id`, `severity` (`critical` | `high` | `medium` | `low`), `check` (`completeness` | `validity` | `uniqueness`), `dataset` (topic), `source_message_id`, `source_department`, `department`, `due_day` (raised day plus the severity SLA), `summary`. Audience: Operations (process fixes) and Product (telemetry fixes).

### SLA and controls

- Resolve within: critical 1 day, high 2, medium 5, low 10. `dq_issue_mttr_days` target 5 days in FY2026 (3 in FY2027, 2 in FY2028); `data_quality_pass_rate` target 95% weekly.
- Quarantine decisions are logged. No source record is ever altered by Data.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Pass rate below 95% for two consecutive months triggers the Senior Data Engineer hiring trigger (`blueprint.md` §5).
- Suspected exposure of client A data to client B scope: critical, SOP-DATA-11 incident branch; the Chief Data and AI Officer informs the CISO immediately (data breach chain, COMPANY.md §9.3).

## SOP-DATA-09: AI agent register and quality reporting

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (Principal Data Architect, owner of the Agent Quality Evaluation Agent, from FY2028 Q1), jointly with the CISO for the register (COMPANY.md §9.4).
**Trigger**: `product.ai_risk_review_requested`, `legal.ai_risk_review_completed`, and the quarterly schedule (January, April, July, October)
**Inputs**: `agent_name` (or `agent_id`, `agent`, `review_id`), review `outcome`, the agent register, evaluation harness results (accuracy, escalation rate, policy breaches).

### Steps

1. [agent] Review requested: add the agent to the register in status `under_review` (once) and create a task (7 days) to add it to the evaluation harness.
2. [agent] Review completed: set status `approved`, or `rejected` if the outcome starts with `reject`; publish a post-review report for that agent.
3. [human] The accountable owner of the agent confirms the evaluation result. A rejected agent is never enabled; the Chief Data and AI Officer tells the agent owner and the CISO. Data cannot disable an agent itself.
4. [agent] Quarterly: always publish the quarterly agent quality report, including when the register is empty (zero agents registered, coverage blank): registered, evaluated (status other than `under_review`), coverage %, agents under review, agents rejected.
5. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer reviews coverage with the CISO; agents not evaluated in 30 days are scheduled.

### Outputs and messages

- `data.agent_quality_report_published`: `report_id`, `report_type` (`quarterly` | `post_review`), `agents_registered`, `agents_evaluated`, `coverage_pct`, `under_review`, `rejected`, and for post-review reports `agent` and `outcome`, `summary`. Audience: Product, Delivery, Executive.
- Task: add the agent to the evaluation harness (7 days).

### SLA and controls

- `agent_eval_coverage` target 80% in FY2026, 95% FY2027, 100% FY2028 (agents evaluated in the last 30 days, `kpis.md`). Register covers every agent in COMPANY.md §6.1 headcount (22 in FY2026, 150 by FY2028).
- Material changes to client-facing agents need the AI risk review before release (COMPANY.md §9.4).

### Exceptions and escalation

- A policy breach found in evaluation goes to the agent owner and the CISO the same day, and is flagged in the report.
- No review outcome after 14 days: the Chief Data and AI Officer chases the General Counsel and the Chief Product Officer.

## SOP-DATA-11: Regulatory change, retention and quarterly access review

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (Data Governance Lead from FY2028 Q2, owner of the Governance and Lineage Agent).
**Trigger**: `legal.regulatory_change_noted`, and the quarterly schedule (January, April, July, October)
**Inputs**: Regulatory change `summary` and `severity`; access lists from IT Security; retention schedule; client-data segregation test results.

### Steps

1. [agent] Regulatory change noted: flag a retention review as pending and create a task (14 days) to assess retention, privacy and access impact; priority high when the severity is high or critical, otherwise medium.
2. [human] The Chief Data and AI Officer agrees any change to the retention schedule or classification with the General Counsel, who owns the legal basis and DPIAs.
3. [agent] Quarterly: create a high-priority task (30 days) to complete the access review and the client-data segregation test, and a task (30 days) to review the retention schedule and delete expired datasets.
4. [human] IT Security provisions and removes access; Data owns the policy content and the review. Deletions and access removals proposed by the agent need human approval before execution.
5. [human] Segregation breach branch: contain, tell the CISO and the General Counsel the same day, and follow the customer data breach notification rule (ICO within 72 hours, General Counsel with CISO and CEO, COMPANY.md §9.3). Data does not notify regulators.

### Outputs and messages

- No emitted message; tasks only (regulatory assessment, access review, retention review). The review outcomes are reported in the monthly KPI report (`access_review_completion`, `retention_schedule_compliance`, `client_data_segregation_breaches`).

### SLA and controls

- `access_review_completion` target 100% each quarter; `retention_schedule_compliance` 95% in FY2026; `client_data_segregation_breaches` target 0.
- Retention schedule reviewed at least quarterly and on any regulatory change.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Overdue access review at day 30: escalate to the CISO and the CEO.
- Conflict between legal retention and client contractual deletion rights: Legal decides.

## SOP-DATA-12: Metric registry and dashboard certification

**Owner**: Chief Data and AI Officer (BI and Analytics Analyst from FY2027 Q4 for dashboards; Data Governance Lead from FY2028 Q2 for certification).
**Trigger**: Schedule: first Monday of each month (registry and dashboard review)
**Inputs**: Metric registry (id, formula, source, owner, certification status), dashboard list with owner and review date, `kpis.md` definitions.

### Steps

1. [human] Review new or changed metrics: the formula is peer-reviewed, tested against a hand calculation and signed by the owner before it is certified.
2. [agent] Compute `certified_metric_coverage` (certified metrics in use in executive reporting / metrics in use) and `certified_dashboard_share` (dashboards using only certified metrics / live dashboards).
3. [human] The owner fixes or labels as uncertified any dashboard past its review date.
4. [human] Only the Chief Data and AI Officer may approve a change to a certified definition, and every change is communicated to Finance, Sales and Executive before the next report.
5. [agent] Report both KPIs in the KPI report and flag departments reporting numbers outside the registry.

### Outputs and messages

- Registry and dashboard changes are logged internally; coverage figures are carried in `data.kpi_report_published` (`kpis`). No separate topic is emitted.

### SLA and controls

- `certified_metric_coverage` 80% and `certified_dashboard_share` 70% in FY2026 (90 and 85 FY2027; 95 and 95 FY2028).
- Source precedence when systems disagree: Finance ledger for money, Sales CRM for deal stage, People HR system for headcount, Customer Success platform for health.

### Exceptions and escalation

- Two departments reporting different values for the same metric: the registry definition wins; the Chief Data and AI Officer resolves it with the department heads within 2 working days.
